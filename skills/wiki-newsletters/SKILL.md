# Newsletters Worth Tracking — Legal-Tech and Adjacent

## Scope

This pack covers the newsletters and recurring publications that a legal-AI operator, legal practitioner, or legal-tech team member should track: technology strategy, product and growth, legal-tech, MENA-specific legal and business intelligence, and the regulatory and bar association communications that affect the legal-AI market.

---

## Technology strategy

**Stratechery** (Ben Thompson)
- Focus: technology strategy, business model analysis, market power
- Why follow: the best writing on platform dynamics and AI strategy. His frameworks (bundling/unbundling, aggregation theory) apply directly to how legal-AI products position against established players (LexisNexis, Thomson Reuters)
- Format: 3x/week; paid (worth it)

**Benedict Evans Newsletter** (Ben Evans)
- Focus: macro tech trends, market analysis
- Why follow: broad technology trend analysis; useful for understanding AI adoption curves and how legal-tech fits in the wider tech landscape
- Format: weekly; free

**TLDR** (Dan Ni)
- Focus: daily tech news summary
- Why follow: scanning signal; efficient way to track AI product developments that may affect legal-AI
- Format: daily; free

---

## Product and growth

**Lenny's Newsletter** (Lenny Rachitsky)
- Focus: product management, growth, startup advice; guest interviews with PMs and operators
- Why follow: the highest-quality consistent writing on product strategy and growth in the startup context. Highly applicable to a legal-AI product team
- Format: weekly; free + paid tiers

**Every** (formerly The Generalist)
- Focus: AI-era business building; startup profiles
- Why follow: increasingly focused on AI products; profiles of AI-first companies and their operational approaches
- Format: multiple writers; free + paid

**The Pragmatic Engineer** (Gergely Orosz)
- Focus: software engineering careers and industry; big tech dynamics
- Why follow: the best writing on engineering team dynamics, compensation, and technical career management. Relevant for technical hiring (see [[wiki-hiring]])
- Format: biweekly; free + paid

## Legal-tech

**Artificial Lawyer** (Richard Tromans)
- Focus: global legal-tech industry news; company profiles, funding rounds, product launches
- Why follow: the most comprehensive tracker of the legal-tech market; useful for competitive intelligence (see [[wiki-legal-tech]])
- Format: daily; free

**Legal IT Insider**
- Focus: UK/Europe legal IT and legal-tech
- Why follow: strong coverage of how law firms are adopting technology; buyer-side perspective
- Format: regular; free

**Law.com / ALM Intelligence**
- Focus: US legal industry; law firm business, Am Law rankings, lateral moves
- Why follow: US market context; useful for understanding how global law firms (with MENA offices) are thinking about AI adoption
- Format: daily; some free, subscription for full access

**Lex Machina Newsletter / Litigation Analytics newsletters**
- Focus: data-driven legal analytics; US courts
- Why follow: illustrates how data can transform legal practice; useful framing for MENA data analytics opportunities

**Casetext (now CoCounsel) content**
- Historical context: Casetext produced some of the best early writing on practical AI in legal workflows before acquisition by Thomson Reuters. Their published case studies and blog posts remain useful reference material for how to communicate AI value to legal professionals

## MENA legal and business intelligence

**Al-Monitor**
- Focus: MENA politics, economics, policy
- Why follow: the best English-language regional intelligence source; geopolitical context for legal practitioners

**MEED (Middle East Economic Digest)**
- Focus: infrastructure, energy, project finance in MENA
- Why follow: project finance and infrastructure legal work in GCC; understanding client base

**Zawya / Reuters MENA**
- Focus: MENA business news
- Why follow: daily deal flow, regulatory announcements, economic data

**DIFC Academy / ADGM Academy publications**
- Focus: regulatory updates, legal education, practice notes from the free zones
- Why follow: primary source for DIFC/ADGM regulatory changes; essential for practitioners advising on financial centre matters

**UAE Official Gazette (Al-Jarida Al-Rasmiyya)**
- Not a newsletter — but monitoring new legislation via the official gazette is essential for any practitioner advising on UAE law. Set up alerts or subscribe to a service that monitors gazette publications

**KSA Umm Al-Qura (Official Gazette)**
- Same principle as UAE; primary source for Royal Decrees and ministerial decisions

## Running a legal-tech newsletter

If the strategy includes publishing a regular newsletter (see [[wiki-content]] for content strategy and [[wiki-medium]] for platform options), key principles:

- **One clear topic per issue**: do not write a generic "here's what happened this week" roundup. Pick one topic and go deep — a 600-word analysis of a specific regulatory change is more useful to a practitioner than 200 words on five topics.
- **Jurisdiction-specific versions**: UAE subscribers care about DHA rule changes; KSA subscribers care about ZATCA updates. Segmented newsletters outperform generic MENA digests.
- **Bilingual where practical**: an Arabic-language summary at the top of each issue extends reach into Arabic-primary markets without requiring a full translation.
- **Consistent send day and time**: Wednesday mornings (Gulf Standard Time) is a high-open-rate window for professional newsletters in GCC; avoid Friday (prayer time) and Saturday.
- **Plain text wins**: legal professionals tend to prefer clean, text-heavy emails over heavily designed HTML newsletters. A plain text format also renders better on mobile.

---

## Caveats & currency

Newsletter ecosystems change. Publications shut down, get acquired, pivot, or reduce quality. The list above reflects high-quality sources as of early 2026; check publication status and recent output before recommending any specific newsletter to a practitioner. MENA-specific legal-tech newsletters are sparse; the gap is an opportunity for a MENA legal-AI product to fill by launching its own.
