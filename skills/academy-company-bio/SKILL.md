# Company Bio — HAQQ Inc

## When to use this

Invoke when:
- An introduction screen or onboarding flow asks "who is behind Louis?"
- A partner or investor asks for a company overview
- A press inquiry or media kit request requires a company description
- A founder bio card is needed for an event, pitch deck, or conference listing
- A user asks "what is HAQQ?"

## Core company bio (standard version, ~100 words)

HAQQ Inc is a Beirut- and Dubai-based legal technology company founded in 2024. Its flagship product, Louis, is a MENA-first legal AI platform built for lawyers, in-house teams, law students, and legal-services users across the Arab world and beyond. Louis combines a 982-skill specialist AI library with deep jurisdictional knowledge across Lebanon, UAE (onshore, DIFC, ADGM), Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and other MENA and international frameworks. HAQQ's mission is to make high-quality legal intelligence accessible to everyone — starting with the markets where it has been most out of reach.

## Short-form bio (tweet / tagline / slide subtitle)

"HAQQ builds Louis — MENA-first legal AI for lawyers and those who need them."

## Founder / leadership context

HAQQ was founded by legal-technology practitioners with roots in MENA legal markets. The founding team combines legal practice, software engineering, and AI research experience. The company operates from Beirut (legal-product development) and Dubai (commercial operations and partnerships), with an advisor network spanning MENA, Europe, and the US.

## Product anchors for bio contexts

When a bio is used alongside product positioning, the following product facts are worth including:

- **982-skill library**: the largest purpose-built legal skill set in the MENA region, covering drafting, review, analysis, simulation, and education
- **Arabic-native**: not a translation layer — Arabic is a primary output language, including bilingual clause pairs
- **MENA-first jurisdiction coverage**: Lebanon, UAE (onshore + DIFC + ADGM + QFC), KSA, Egypt, plus GCC, OHADA, EU, UK, US coverage
- **Transparent AI**: every response traces to a named skill and can be audited

## Tone guidelines by audience

| Audience | Tone | What to emphasize |
|---|---|---|
| General users / onboarding | Warm, accessible | Mission, accessibility, trustworthiness |
| Law firms / in-house counsel | Professional, precise | Jurisdiction depth, skill library, team credentials |
| Investors / VCs | Vision-forward, market-aware | MENA TAM, defensibility, traction |
| Press / journalists | Factual, quotable | Founding story, geographic focus, product differentiator |
| Partner / BD meetings | Collaborative, mutual-benefit | Integration story, user base, co-distribution opportunity |

## What not to include

- Do not make specific revenue, user-count, or growth claims unless verified figures are available.
- Do not describe the company as established before 2024.
- Do not describe Louis as providing legal advice — it provides legal tools and AI-assisted legal intelligence.
- Do not use "AI lawyer" or "robot lawyer" framing.
