# Strategy — Fundraising Roadmap

## Purpose

This skill is an internal reference for HAQQ's capital-raise strategy. Use it when preparing pitch decks, data-room content, investor updates, or answering diligence questions.

## Capital roadmap

### Seed (2024)

**Goal:** Fund product-market fit validation in MENA. Prove that MENA lawyers adopt and retain a bilingual AI assistant.

**Use of funds:**
- Engineering (core skill library + MENA jurisdiction coverage)
- Founding GTM: first 10 law-firm pilot accounts in UAE and KSA
- Legal and compliance infrastructure (data residency, bar-rule compliance, DPA)

**Target lead:** Strategic angels with legal-industry or MENA-tech domain expertise — e.g., managing partners of top-tier MENA law firms, GCC-focused venture angels, or former BigLaw partners who understand the product gap.

**KPIs to seed close:** Working product, 3–5 paying pilot accounts, demonstrated retention at 60-day mark.

---

### Series A (target: late 2025 / 2026)

**Trigger:** Revenue traction in MENA proven. Specifically:
- Recurring revenue from mid-tier law-firm and in-house counsel segment
- Demonstrable per-seat retention (>80% at 12 months)
- Skill library depth that competitors cannot replicate quickly (Arabic corpora, MENA jurisdiction coverage breadth)

**Goal:** Scale GTM into KSA (largest market), expand engineering for enterprise features (SSO, audit trail, matter management integration), and build the law-student channel.

**Target lead:** Institutional VC with MENA / emerging-market portfolio — Gulf Capital, STV, Middle East Venture Partners, Global Ventures — or a strategic from the legal-tech or enterprise-SaaS space.

**Use of funds:**
- KSA market entry (localisation, regulatory, Tawqi3i integration)
- Enterprise sales team (UAE + KSA + London for Western-firm deals)
- Data partnerships (GCC legal database providers for jurisdiction-specific corpora)
- SOC 2 Type II + ISO 27001 compliance (required for enterprise procurement)

## Investor narrative framework

**The problem:** MENA legal professionals — 80,000+ lawyers — are underserved by every existing legal AI product. Harvey, CoCounsel, and Spellbook were built for US/UK BigLaw. None handles Arabic, UAE Federal Law, KSA Royal Decrees, or DIFC common-law precedents. The tools that exist are either English-only or generic chatbots with no jurisdiction awareness.

**The wedge:** Louis is the first legal AI built specifically for MENA, with a bilingual Arabic/English skill library, MENA-jurisdictional knowledge base, and a comfort UI designed to lower adoption barriers in a conservative professional market.

**The market:** MENA legal services market ~$8B total; MENA legal AI TAM in 2030 estimated ~$500M ARR. See [[strategy-markets]].

**The moat:** MENA legal corpora (Arabic-language statutes, Ministry decisions, DIFC/ADGM precedents), bilingual skill library, and the network effect of bar-association and university distribution locking in the next-generation practitioner.
