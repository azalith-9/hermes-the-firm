# Press List — MENA

MENA press coverage is the highest-credibility signal for a legal AI product serving MENA lawyers. A placement in a leading UAE or KSA business publication reaches the exact professional audience — GCs, managing partners, legal operations heads — that adopts and recommends legal AI tools. This skill provides the MENA press target list and the pitch strategy for each segment.

## Pitch strategy by segment

### UAE/GCC business press

Angle: "AI making legal more accessible and affordable for the GCC's growth companies."
- Frame around UAE Vision 2031, NEOM, Saudi Giga Projects — the growth context
- Cite a specific use case: contract review time, cost reduction, multi-language support
- Offer a UAE-based GC or managing partner as a spokesperson

### Saudi press

Angle: "Saudi National Transformation Programme needs legal infrastructure — AI plays a role."
- Conservative, credibility-first framing
- Avoid controversy around AI + Sharia law in the pitch (engage it thoughtfully in the article)
- Arabic-language version of the pitch for Arabic publications — use a bilingual spokesperson

### Lebanese / Levant press

Angle: "A tool built with the MENA legal system's complexity in mind — including Lebanon's mixed civil/common law tradition."
- Emphasise French civil-law coverage (Lebanon uses a French-derived civil code)
- The diaspora story: MENA lawyers practicing internationally using a MENA-fluent tool
- L'Orient Today and Executive Magazine both take long-form feature pitches

### MENA legal publications

Angle: "Open-source AI skills library for MENA legal professionals — free to use."
- Lead with the open-source / free access framing — unusual in legal AI
- Offer contributed articles (legal analysis authored by the team) for backlink-rich placements
- Lexology and Chambers are particularly valuable for SEO and credibility

## Timing

Key MEAD press events: World Government Summit (February, Dubai), GITEX (October, Dubai), Saudi Vision forum (October, Riyadh), Arab Legal Forum. Plan major placements to coincide with these events — journalists are in the market for regional tech stories.

Ramadan: publication activity reduces significantly. Avoid major pitch campaigns during Ramadan.

## Arabic-language outreach

For Arabic-language publications:
- Pitch in Arabic — even a translated version of an English pitch is significantly better received than English-only
- Use formal Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), not a specific dialect
- The product must have an Arabic-language interface or at minimum Arabic FAQ to be credible in Arabic press coverage
