# Strategy — Competitive Landscape

## Purpose

This skill is an internal reference for positioning Louis against the existing legal AI competitive set. Use it when preparing investor materials, sales battle cards, partnership pitches, or product-positioning decisions.

## Competitive map

### Tier 1 — Well-funded US-centric platforms

| Competitor | Focus | Funding signal | Key strength | Key weakness vs Louis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Harvey** | BigLaw US (Am Law 100) | $100M+ series B | Deep US case law, enterprise security posture | No MENA coverage; English-only; extremely expensive for mid-market |
| **CoCounsel (TR)** | Westlaw-integrated, US enterprise | Thomson Reuters subsidiary | Bound Westlaw citations, deposition summaries | US-law-only; no Arabic; no MENA jurisdiction knowledge |

### Tier 2 — Mid-market / SMB

| Competitor | Focus | Funding signal | Key strength | Key weakness vs Louis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Spellbook** | Contract drafting, mid-market | Series A (est.) | Fast NDA / clause drafting; Word add-in UX | Primarily North American law; no MENA |
| **Robin AI** | Contract review + management | Series B (UK) | Strong UK + EU coverage | Limited MENA; Arabic support absent |

### Tier 3 — Adjacent / CLM

| Competitor | Focus | Key strength | Key weakness vs Louis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ironclad** | Contract lifecycle management (CLM) | Enterprise workflow automation, integrations | Not an AI drafting tool; no legal research; no MENA localisation |
| **Genie AI** | Consumer + template library | Free-tier templates, consumer-friendly | Template library, not an intelligent drafter; UK-biased; no MENA |

## HAQQ's differentiated wedge

Louis wins on **four axes** that no competitor currently addresses together:

1. **MENA jurisdictions** — Native knowledge of UAE (federal + DIFC/ADGM/QFC), KSA, Lebanon, Egypt, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain. Competitors have none or surface-level coverage.
2. **Arabic-first bilingualism** — Documents drafted or reviewed in Arabic, with bilingual outputs. Critical for onshore UAE/KSA compliance where Arabic is the legally controlling language.
3. **Comfort UI** — Designed for lawyers who are not AI-native. Guided, low-intimidation interactions rather than a raw chatbot. Named "Louis" to lower the psychological barrier to AI adoption in a conservative professional sector.
4. **Skill-router transparency** — Users see which skill is active for their task. Builds trust and legal-professional comfort with AI-assisted work — a regulatory and reputational advantage as bar associations develop AI ethics guidance.

## Competitive risks to monitor

- Harvey or CoCounsel acquiring a MENA legal data provider and entering the market.
- A GCC sovereign fund backing a local competitor (Badr, LexMENA, etc.) with a government-data moat.
- Regional incumbents (e.g., MenaRA, Lexis+MENA) adding AI layers to existing legal databases.
- Microsoft Copilot for Legal deepening Arabic + MENA coverage via Azure/OpenAI.

## Positioning do-nots

- Do not position Louis as a "cheaper Harvey" — different market, different buyer.
- Do not lead with "AI" in sales conversations; lead with the job-to-be-done ("draft an NDA under UAE law in 4 minutes").
- Do not over-claim coverage in jurisdictions where Louis skills are still thin.
