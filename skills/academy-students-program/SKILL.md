# Students Program — Free Legal AI for Law Students

## When to use this

Invoke when:
- A law student asks "is there a student plan?"
- A law school administrator asks about integrating Louis into their curriculum
- A student asks about becoming a campus ambassador
- A student participating in a moot court or legal writing competition asks for support
- A law student working on a thesis asks about research assistance

## Program overview

HAQQ's Students Program provides **free access to Justinian** (HAQQ's legal education sub-product) for verified law students, alongside a campus community program designed to build a generation of AI-literate legal professionals.

## What the program includes

### Free Justinian Access
Verified law students receive full access to Justinian at no cost for the duration of their legal studies:
- Bar exam preparation tools (jurisdiction-selectable)
- IRAC case brief generator and coach
- Moot court rehearsal (Socratic judge simulation)
- Course outline generator
- Study planning and time management coaching

Access covers the educational features of Justinian; professional drafting and review features of Louis are available at a discounted student rate.

### Eligibility
- Enrolled in an accredited law school or faculty of law
- Active student status (re-verified annually)
- Any jurisdiction; MENA-based universities prioritized for on-campus partnership programs

### Campus Ambassador Program
Selected student ambassadors represent HAQQ at their law school:
- Organize Louis and Justinian workshops for fellow students
- Provide feedback to the HAQQ product team from the student perspective
- Earn a monthly stipend and enhanced Louis access (including professional features)
- Build a portfolio of legal technology experience relevant to a future legal career

Ambassador selection criteria:
- Active law student (2L or above preferred)
- Strong communication skills; ability to run a 60-minute workshop
- Interest in legal technology and AI

### Student Competitions
HAQQ co-sponsors legal AI and legal technology competitions:
- **Louis Legal AI Challenge**: teams solve a real legal scenario using Louis tools; judged on quality of legal analysis and effective use of AI
- **Justinian Moot**: a moot court competition with AI-assisted preparation phase
- **Legal Hackathon**: engineering + law teams build legal tools on the Louis API

Prizes include cash awards, professional mentoring sessions, and guaranteed interview access with HAQQ partner law firms.

### Thesis Support
Law students writing theses on topics related to legal technology, AI and law, MENA legal systems, or related subjects can apply for:
- Extended Justinian and Louis access for research purposes
- Optional informal advisory sessions with the HAQQ legal and research team
- Co-authorship opportunities (where the research is publishable and HAQQ researchers contributed substantively)

## Application process

1. Sign up at [Louis student program page] with university email and student ID
2. HAQQ verifies student status (within 2 business days for most institutions)
3. Approved students receive Justinian credentials
4. Campus ambassador applications: submit a short statement of interest + 1 idea for a campus event

## For law schools and faculties

HAQQ offers law schools a group enrollment pathway:
- Verified student batch onboarding (no individual applications needed)
- Curriculum integration support for the Learn Legal with AI program
- Faculty access for professors who want to incorporate Justinian exercises into coursework
- Workshop facilitation by HAQQ team members (virtual or in-person at selected partner institutions)

Interested institutions should contact the HAQQ academic partnerships team (see [[academy-partnership-pitch]]).

## What the program is not

- Justinian is a learning tool, not a legal advice service. Student outputs are not suitable for filing in any legal proceeding or providing to a client.
- The program does not provide bar admission or law degree credit; CLE/CPD credit for the Learn Legal with AI curriculum is being pursued separately.
- Ambassador stipends are modest (program recognition, not employment); ambassadors are not HAQQ employees.
