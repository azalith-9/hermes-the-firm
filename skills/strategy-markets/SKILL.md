# Strategy — Markets

## Purpose

This skill defines HAQQ's market scope and sequencing. Use it when making prioritisation decisions about which markets to invest in, which features to build first, and how to frame the opportunity for investors.

## Core market: MENA legal AI

**Total addressable market (MENA legal AI, 2030): ~$500M ARR**

This estimate derives from:
- ~$8B total MENA legal services revenue
- Projected 6–8% penetration by AI tools at meaningful per-seat spend
- Average contract value: $600–1,500/seat/year

**HAQQ target share: 30–40%** of the MENA legal AI TAM — i.e., $150–200M ARR by 2030 — achieved by:
1. First-mover advantage: no viable bilingual MENA legal AI competitor exists today
2. Distribution moat: bar-association + university channels lock in the practitioner pipeline
3. Language barrier to entry: Arabic-language legal corpora are expensive and slow to build; competitors who start now face a 2–3 year lag

## Market entry sequencing

| Phase | Markets | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (now)** | UAE, KSA | Largest revenue; English+Arabic bilingual; DIFC/ADGM international hub; Vision 2030 regulatory wave |
| **Phase 2 (2025–2026)** | Lebanon, Egypt, Qatar | Lebanon: high legal-professional density; LB civil law skills already in library. Egypt: largest population, underserved. Qatar: QFC hub. |
| **Phase 3 (2026+)** | Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan | Smaller markets; GCC legal systems close to UAE federal law; lower marginal cost to add |

## Adjacent markets

### Francophone Africa (OHADA zone)

**Opportunity:** OHADA (Organisation pour l'Harmonisation en Afrique du Droit des Affaires) governs commercial law across 17 West and Central African countries — Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, DR Congo, and others. Combined legal services market is growing rapidly as these economies formalise.

**Louis's advantage:** French-language capability, civil-law knowledge base, and OHADA's harmonised commercial code (which overlaps structurally with Lebanese/Syrian/Egyptian civil-law traditions) transfer well.

**Trigger to enter:** When MENA revenue reaches Series A threshold and engineering capacity is available for a dedicated OHADA skill library.

### MENA–Europe diaspora deals

**Opportunity:** High-value M&A and investment transactions where GCC family offices, sovereign wealth funds, or Lebanese/Egyptian holding structures pair with European (French, UK, Belgian) counterparties. These deals need both MENA and EU/UK law simultaneously — a use case no single competitor handles.

**Louis's advantage:** Existing MENA jurisdiction depth + EU/UK skill coverage (DIFC/ADGM common law, EUR-Lex, UK Companies House) combine to cover both sides of these transactions.

**Go-to-market:** International law firms handling Gulf-origin M&A in London and Paris are the distribution channel.

## Market-sizing methodology note

All figures are estimates based on public industry research and analyst reports. Before using in an investor presentation, verify against:
- Legal500 MENA market reports
- LexisNexis / TR emerging-market legal services data
- GCC bar association membership statistics (as a proxy for addressable practitioner count)
