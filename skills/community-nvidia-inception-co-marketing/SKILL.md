# NVIDIA Inception Co-Marketing — Campaign and Activation Guide

## When to use this

Invoke when:
- HAQQ needs to activate its NVIDIA Inception membership for marketing purposes
- A conference presentation or joint case study is being prepared with NVIDIA's involvement
- A press release needs to reference the Inception program membership
- A potential partner asks about HAQQ's technology ecosystem and NVIDIA credentials
- The HAQQ team wants to apply for compute credits or access Inception-specific benefits

## What NVIDIA Inception is

NVIDIA Inception is a program designed to support AI startups through access to:
- Technical resources (compute credits, NVIDIA DGX Cloud access, developer tools)
- Co-marketing opportunities (joint press, conference co-presentations, social amplification)
- Network access (other Inception portfolio companies, NVIDIA enterprise sales connections)
- Technical validation (NVIDIA's implicit endorsement as a member adds credibility)

Membership is non-exclusive and does not involve equity or formal partnership agreements in most cases. It signals NVIDIA's recognition of the company's technical credibility in AI.

## Joint case studies

### What makes a strong HAQQ/NVIDIA joint case study

The case study should:
- Demonstrate a concrete use of NVIDIA infrastructure or models in HAQQ's product (e.g., inference acceleration, model deployment on NVIDIA hardware)
- Quantify the result: latency improvement, throughput, cost efficiency, or legal outcome quality
- Feature a real customer (with consent) or a benchmark scenario with verifiable methodology
- Include an NVIDIA spokesperson or quote if coordinated through the Inception team

**Structure for a joint case study:**

1. **Challenge** (1 paragraph): what problem HAQQ's customers face that the AI capability addresses
2. **HAQQ solution** (1 paragraph): what Louis does to address it
3. **NVIDIA technology** (1 paragraph): which NVIDIA platform, SDK, or infrastructure underpins the solution
4. **Results** (quantified where possible): processing speed, accuracy, cost, scale
5. **Quote from HAQQ team** (CEO or CTO level)
6. **Quote or validation from NVIDIA Inception team** (if available)
7. **Call to action**: link to Louis free trial + Inception membership page

### Approval process
All case study content referencing NVIDIA must be reviewed by HAQQ's NVIDIA Inception contact before publication. Allow 5–7 business days for NVIDIA review. Do not claim NVIDIA's endorsement of specific legal AI outcomes or legal judgments.

## Conference co-presentations

HAQQ can apply through NVIDIA Inception for:
- Speaking slots at NVIDIA GTC (GPU Technology Conference)
- Side-event speaking at major AI and legal tech conferences where NVIDIA has a presence (e.g., AI Summit, GITEX, Legal Tech conferences in Dubai and Riyadh)
- Panel co-presentations on "AI in professional services" themes

**Presentation structure for NVIDIA Inception co-presentations:**

| Section | Content | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Opening: the opportunity | Why legal AI in MENA is a significant AI opportunity | 5 min |
| HAQQ's technical approach | How Louis is built: skill architecture, inference, multilingual AI | 8 min |
| NVIDIA technology layer | How NVIDIA infrastructure enables scale and performance | 5 min |
| Live demo or case study | Real use case with NVIDIA-accelerated performance | 7 min |
| Takeaways and Q&A | What the audience can do next; Inception program mention | 5 min |

Avoid making the presentation feel like a joint advertisement. The technical content should stand on its own merit.

## Technology validation press

NVIDIA Inception membership is a legitimate third-party validation signal for:
- Press releases about HAQQ funding rounds or product launches
- Partnership announcements that benefit from a technology credibility anchor
- Website "trusted by / backed by" sections

**How to reference Inception correctly:**
- "HAQQ is an NVIDIA Inception member" ✓
- "NVIDIA-backed HAQQ" ✗ (Inception is not an investment program; do not imply equity relationship)
- "NVIDIA-validated AI" ✗ (Inception membership does not constitute NVIDIA's technical validation of specific claims)
- "Built on NVIDIA infrastructure" — only if factually accurate for the specific deployment

## Compute credits

NVIDIA Inception members may be eligible for compute credits (NVIDIA DGX Cloud, NGC access, API credits). These can be applied to:
- Model fine-tuning experiments
- Inference infrastructure for production workloads
- Development and testing environments

Apply for credits through the Inception member portal. Credits are typically tiered by company stage (startup vs. scale-up) and are subject to NVIDIA's current program terms.

## Inception alumni network

The Inception network gives HAQQ access to:
- Other Inception portfolio companies (potential integration partners, co-marketing allies, mutual customers)
- NVIDIA enterprise sales connections (potential channel for HAQQ in large enterprise accounts where NVIDIA already has relationships)
- NVIDIA research and developer advocacy teams

Best practices for network activation:
- Attend NVIDIA-hosted Inception company meetups (virtual and in-person at GTC)
- Introduce HAQQ in the Inception community forums with a clear "what we do / what partnerships we're looking for" statement
- Identify 3–5 non-competing Inception companies in adjacent verticals (legal tech, fintech, GovTech) for potential co-marketing
