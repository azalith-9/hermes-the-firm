# Venture Capital Ecosystem Reference

## Scope

This pack covers the venture capital ecosystem: fund structures, major global and MENA-specific funds, investment thesis frameworks, deal mechanics, and investor rights. It is relevant to startup counsel, M&A lawyers advising VC-backed companies, and founders navigating the fundraising process.

---

## VC Fund Structure

### How a VC Fund Works

A typical VC fund is structured as a limited partnership:
- **General Partners (GPs)**: manage the fund; make investment decisions; carry fiduciary duties to LPs
- **Limited Partners (LPs)**: provide the capital; have limited liability; include pension funds, endowments, sovereign wealth funds, family offices, fund of funds, corporations
- **Management fees**: typically 2% per annum on committed capital during the investment period; covers fund operations
- **Carried interest ("carry")**: typically 20% of profits after LPs receive their capital back (plus hurdle rate if applicable); GP's primary economic incentive
- **Hurdle rate**: minimum return LPs must receive before GP takes carry (typical: 8% IRR)
- **Fund life**: typically 10 years (2-year extension possible); investment period first 5 years

### Fund Types by Stage

| Fund type | Stage | Check size | Key metrics |
|-----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| Pre-seed / angel | Idea to MVP | $25k–$500k | Team quality; market size |
| Seed fund | MVP to early traction | $500k–$3M | PMF signals; early revenue |
| Series A fund | PMF to scaling | $5M–$15M | Unit economics; go-to-market |
| Growth equity | Proven model, scale-up | $20M–$100M+ | Revenue growth rate; NRR |
| Late-stage / pre-IPO | Pre-public, large scale | $50M–$500M+ | Path to profitability; market position |

## VC Investment Terms — Legal Reference

### Key Economic Terms

| Term | Definition | Founder implication |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Pre-money valuation | Company value before the investment | $10M pre + $2M investment = $2M/12M = 16.7% dilution |
| Post-money valuation | Pre-money + investment amount | Used to calculate investor ownership |
| Liquidation preference | VCs get X× their investment back before founders see proceeds | 1× non-participating = founder-friendly; 2× or participating = aggressive |
| Participating preferred | VC gets preference AND shares remaining proceeds pro-rata | Double-dips; avoid if possible |
| Anti-dilution | Adjusts VC's ownership if a down round occurs | Full ratchet = very aggressive; broad-based WA = standard |
| Option pool | Reserved equity for future employee grants | "Option pool shuffle" — investors often require pre-money option pool expansion, increasing founder dilution |

### Key Control Terms

| Term | Definition | Founder implication |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Board seat | Investor representative on the board | Protect governance; maintain founder majority until Series B |
| Protective provisions | Investor veto rights on specific actions | Standard: new equity, M&A, debt, change of business — watch for overly broad lists |
| Drag-along | Forces minority shareholders to sell if majority approves | Ensure threshold is majority of all shares, not just preferred |
| Information rights | Investor's right to financial reporting | Standard; monthly/quarterly financials, annual audited |
| Pro-rata rights | Right to invest in future rounds to maintain ownership % | Valuable for VCs; fine for founders |
| Right of first refusal (ROFR) | Investor right to buy shares if founder wants to sell | Standard; negotiate carve-outs for estate planning transfers |

### MENA-Specific VC Legal Considerations

- **Sharia-compliant structures**: some MENA investors (particularly GCC sovereign funds) require Sharia-compliant investment structures; standard US-style preferred shares may need adaptation (avoiding riba — fixed guaranteed return)
- **Foreign ownership restrictions**: UAE-mainland companies historically required UAE national ownership (51%); reforms under the Companies Law 2015 amendments (and further liberalization) have reduced restrictions in many sectors — verify current rules for the specific sector
- **Exit routes in MENA**: IPO exits (DFM, ADX, TADAWUL, EGX) are an option but market depth is shallower than NASDAQ; M&A exits to regional or international acquirers more common; secondary sales to growth funds
- **Currency risk**: MENA investments often in USD; if company revenues are in local currency (especially EGP, LBP), this creates FX exposure that investors and founders should address in financial planning

## VC Fund Legal Documents

For legal professionals advising on VC transactions, the key document set includes:

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Term sheet | Non-binding summary of deal economics and governance | 
| Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) | Definitive agreement for share issuance; reps & warranties |
| Shareholders' Agreement | Governs rights and obligations of all shareholders |
| Articles of Association (amendment) | Bakes preferred share rights into the company's constitution |
| Investors' Rights Agreement | Information rights, registration rights, pro-rata |
| Right of First Refusal & Co-Sale Agreement | Share transfer restrictions |
| Management Rights Letter | Required by some LPs (ERISA-exempt VCs) |
| Legal opinion | Company counsel opinion on due authorization, valid issuance |
| Disclosure schedule | Carve-outs from representations and warranties |
