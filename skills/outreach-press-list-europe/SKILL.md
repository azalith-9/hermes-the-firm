# Press List — Europe

European legal and legal-tech press is valuable for a MENA-focused legal AI product for three reasons: European investors and law firms with MENA practices read it; European editorial coverage establishes credibility for a London or Paris expansion; and many European publications have strong DR scores that carry backlink value. This skill provides a working list of European press targets and the pitch strategy for each segment.

## Pitch strategy by segment

### UK legal press

Angle: "MENA is a growth market for UK-trained lawyers — here's the AI tool built for that practice."
- Emphasise common-law continuity (DIFC, ADGM use English law)
- Cite the number of UK law firms with MENA offices
- Offer a named UK-licensed lawyer as a spokesperson

### French legal press

Angle: "The Franco-Lebanese and Franco-UAE legal relationship — AI that understands both civil-law traditions."
- French-language skills library is a differentiator
- Cite the volume of French investment and legal activity in Lebanon and UAE
- Offer French-language editorial (not just translated content)

### Pan-European / international

Angle: "Open-source AI skills library for cross-border legal practice — free for any jurisdiction."
- Lead with the open-source angle — unusual for legal AI
- Cite the breadth of jurisdictions covered (MENA + EU + common law)
- Offer data on what legal questions users ask most

## Contact approach

For UK publications with named editorial contacts: email the specific journalist (not a generic press@ address).

For French publications: a French-language pitch dramatically improves response rates — translate and personalise.

For pan-European: most accept English pitches; attach a one-page product brief in PDF.

For all: the pitch must have a specific story hook, not just "we have a product." The hook for European press:
- A European law firm's MENA expansion story
- A cross-border deal where multi-jurisdiction AI made a difference
- The open-source skills library release as a news event
- A research finding about MENA legal market growth

## Timing

European legal press has two high-readership periods: January–March (post-holiday, year-in-review commentary) and September–November (conference season — GITEX, Legal Week conferences, IBA Annual Conference). Plan major placements for these windows.
